Planning and maintenance

Solar system guide

1. Assess your loads

List appliances, their rated power and expected operating hours. Estimate daily energy use in watt-hours or kilowatt-hours.

2. Consider autonomy

Decide how long the battery system should support essential loads when solar generation is unavailable.

3. Check compatibility

Verify inverter, battery, PV voltage and current limits against the equipment manufacturers' specifications.

4. Design for safety

Use correctly rated cables, overcurrent protection, isolation and earthing. Installation should be completed or checked by a qualified professional.
